Young Lee (Young Joong Lee)

I am a Ph.D. student in EECS and CSAIL at MIT, advised by Prof. Jas Brooks in the Perceptual Engineering Lab. Previously at MIT, I worked with Prof. Wojciech Matusik and Prof. Giovanni Traverso, and completed my S.M. in EECS in 2025. I received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Northwestern University in 2022, where I worked with Prof. John A. Rogers and Prof. Hyoyoung Jeong.

My research explores how machines can perceive the world in ways that are meaningful to people. Working across sensing systems, embodied AI, and human-robot interaction, I build multimodal sensing systems whose interpretation, reasoning, and actions are grounded in human perception. I am currently focused on smell, with the goal of bringing olfaction into machine perception alongside vision, language, and touch.
